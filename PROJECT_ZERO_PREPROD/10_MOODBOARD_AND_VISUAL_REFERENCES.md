# PROJECT ZERO — MOODBOARD & VISUAL REFERENCES (DOCUMENT 10)

> **Status:** Pre-Production (Planning Only) — **No code.**

## 0. How to Read This Document
This document is not a list of random websites. It is a curated **visual language specification**.

Each category includes:
- Visual goal
- Emotional purpose
- Why it fits PROJECT ZERO
- Things to avoid

---

## 1. Architecture
### Visual goal
- Futuristic **architectural frames** and **monolithic structures**.
- “Systems” feel like legal/engineering infrastructure.

### Emotional purpose
- Establish authority and craft.
- Communicate engineering as stable but evolving.

### Why it fits PROJECT ZERO
- Your story is about backend and AI systems; the architecture should be the interface.
- Architecture must feel like a physical environment, not UI.

### Things to avoid
- Grid-based dashboards
- Stock “sci-fi HUD panels”
- Neon tech overlays

---

## 2. Lighting
### Visual goal
- Premium, restrained bloom.
- Soft key light + rim separation.
- Volumetric lighting only when it increases meaning.

### Emotional purpose
- Lighting directs attention and emotion state.

### Why it fits
- Engineering clarity should look cinematic.
- Each project has unique lighting behavior per the art bible.

### Things to avoid
- Overexposed scenes
- Constant god rays
- Neon-only lighting

---

## 3. Typography
### Visual goal
- Monumental, cinematic type.
- Letters emerge from fog/light like physical matter.

### Emotional purpose
- Make typography the hero and the “camera cut anchor.”

### Why it fits
- Storytelling without paragraphs relies on typographic presence.

### Things to avoid
- Slide-in animations
- Type as decoration
- UI-like small labels everywhere

---

## 4. Composition
### Visual goal
- Large negative space.
- Clear hero anchor.
- Eye guidance via geometry + light.

### Emotional purpose
- Create breathing moments and a film-like reading order.

### Why it fits
- Prevents “website feeling” and keeps focus cinematic.

### Things to avoid
- Card-like layouts
- Random clutter
- Equal visual weight everywhere

---

## 5. Particles
### Visual goal
- Volumetric micro-motes.
- Particle motion is purposeful: drift, fracture, assembly.

### Emotional purpose
- Particles represent uncertainty, transformation, neural pathways.

### Why it fits
- Engineering is visualized through controlled particle physics metaphors.

### Things to avoid
- Fireworks
- Chaotic motion storms
- 2D sprite flatness

---

## 6. Fog
### Visual goal
- Fog as narrative boundary.
- Fog density changes per emotional state.

### Emotional purpose
- Create cinematic transitions without page scrolling.

### Why it fits
- Each scene cut is a fog “travel gate.”

### Things to avoid
- Flat haze covering everything uniformly

---

## 7. Volumetric lighting
### Visual goal
- Light rays only during discovery and climax.

### Emotional purpose
- Increase awe without gimmicks.

### Why it fits
- Premium experience: volumetrics are earned, not constant.

### Things to avoid
- Always-on volumetric noise

---

## 8. Glass materials
### Visual goal
- Translucent planes with subtle internal scattering.

### Emotional purpose
- Glass is used for clarity and boundaries.

### Why it fits
- Used sparingly to avoid “glass everywhere.”

### Things to avoid
- Uncontrolled glowing glass UI

---

## 9. Metal materials
### Visual goal
- Dark brushed alloy.
- Soft specular with realistic anisotropy.

### Emotional purpose
- Stability, security, deterministic reliability.

### Things to avoid
- Chrome mirror looks

---

## 10. Color grading
### Visual goal
- Dark premium grading: deep blacks with restrained accents.
- Accents appear via emissive lights and lighting behavior.

### Emotional purpose
- Maintain timeless AAA atmosphere.

### Things to avoid
- Saturated background color flooding
- Overused neon gradients

---

## 11. Motion language
### Visual goal
- Slow, purposeful motion.
- Emergence and absorption transitions.

### Emotional purpose
- Prevent “website feel.”

### Why it fits
- Motion is narrative explanation, not UI flourish.

### Things to avoid
- Bouncy or chaotic motion
- Random spins

---

## 12. Camera framing
### Visual goal
- Wide establishing → controlled push-ins → climax focus pulls → calm holds.

### Emotional purpose
- Film pacing.

### Why it fits
- Camera is storyteller.

### Things to avoid
- Aggressive rotations
- Handheld chaos

---

## 13. Transitions
### Visual goal
- Fog dissolves + signature object absorption.
- Layer parallax with purposeful speed difference.

### Emotional purpose
- Make every scroll feel like a scene transition.

### Things to avoid
- Fade-only transitions
- Translation-only scrolling

---

## 14. Negative space
### Visual goal
- Silence is visible.
- Use whitespace as composition weight.

### Emotional purpose
- Premium calmness.

### Things to avoid
- Filling empty space with decorative elements

---

## 15. Minimal UI / interactive storytelling
### Visual goal
- “UI” appears only as in-world signage or portal labels.
- Actions are understated.

### Emotional purpose
- The film remains the main product.

### Things to avoid
- Typical navbar/CTA patterns
- SaaS-style button grids
