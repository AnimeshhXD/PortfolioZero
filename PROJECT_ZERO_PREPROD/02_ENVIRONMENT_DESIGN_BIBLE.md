## 0. Document Intent
This document defines the **complete movie-set design** for each environment/world:
- Identity
- LawLens-X
- Triage Assistant
- Workplace Operations Agent
- BenefitBridge
- Mavira OS
- Personal Health Coach
- Ending

Each world is written as a cinematic system:
- narrative (what emotional story it tells)
- atmosphere (mood + weather)
- architecture (structure language)
- weather (fog/rain/light rain/snow-like particles as metaphor)
- particles + fog + sky
- lighting and materials
- audio mood (optional)
- camera feeling
- transition behavior
- interactive behavior (how scroll/mouse changes it)

---

## 1. Shared World Grammar (Applies to every project)
To keep cohesion while allowing radical difference per world.

### 1.1 World Layers (always present)
1. **Foreground Layer:** volumetric particles / micro debris / small fragments
2. **Mid Layer:** project architecture, monoliths, monolithic frameworks, “in-world systems”
3. **Hero Layer:** massive typography or in-world signage emerges later
4. **Screenshots Stage (planar hero imagery):** one or two full-bleed planes max
5. **Fog Boundary:** objects dissolve into fog during transitions
6. **Background Sky:** gradient sky + light motion / subtle rays

### 1.2 Signature Object Evolution (global requirement)
- Signature object must exist in every transition.
- Each project evolves it by changing:
  - material response (glass→metal or metal→stone)
  - emission intensity and color family (project accent)
  - complexity (more “channels”, “seams”, or “threads”)

**Until you choose the exact signature object archetype:**
Use the “signature object” as a conceptual placeholder. In implementation, this maps to a single 3D entity.

## 2. Identity Environment (Scene 1 world)

### Scene / Purpose
**Purpose:** Introduce the visitor to the journey identity—engineering as a calm, intentional craft.

### Emotion
Curiosity → calm authority → readiness to explore.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- Quiet “space-between” atmosphere.
- Light dust in depth.
- Subtle particulate field that never becomes chaotic.

### Architecture
- Futuristic corridor of **floating architectural frames**.
- Walls are made of layered panels that feel like “records,” not UI.
- Hidden network nodes embedded in the architecture.

### Geometry Language
- Curved volumes for transitions (gentle throat-like domes).
- Mostly monolith mass for stability.

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles: micro-motes drifting in layered depth.
- Fog: low density, used mainly as depth boundary.
- Sky: slow animated gradient with barely visible light rays.

### Lighting
- Key light: cool blue-violet, controlled.
- Rim light: outlines corridor depth.
- Bloom: minimal.

### Materials
- Metal (dark alloy) on structural elements.
- Glass only as occasional translucent planes (limited, not everywhere).

### Audio Mood (optional)
- Deep, restrained ambience.
- Subtle low-frequency drone.
- No “synth neon.”

### Camera Feeling
- Slow forward glide.
- Slight vertical drift for “living camera.”
- Gentle focus pull that never aggressively shifts.

### Transition Behavior (to first project)
- Corridor lights dim.
- The hero typography dissolves into particles.
- Fog grows outward like a curtain.
- Signature object becomes visible as the “seed” that pulls the viewer into the next world.

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** soft lighting angle shifts on walls and particles.
- **Scroll:** controls camera push and fog curtain timing, not content translation.

## 3. LawLens-X World (Contract Intelligence)

### Purpose
Emotionally communicate: **contracts become intelligence through agents.**

### Emotion Arc
Mystery → wrongness/fear → reconstruction → reveal → confident clarity.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- A silent space filled with **thousands of floating translucent contract pages**.
- Pages drift like slow snow, but composed and restrained.

### Architecture
- Background architecture: monolithic frame that feels like a legal tribunal.
- The “system” exists as constraints: invisible rails become visible as glowing lines.

### Shape Language
- Start with broken planar fragments (contract clauses).
- Transform fragments into neural pathways (organic energy threads).
- Five agent modules emerge as five entities (visual “actors”), not UI icons.

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles: clause fragments and ink-like motes.
- Fog: thin veil used to hide agents until the emotional reveal.
- Sky: deep cyan-violet gradient.

### Lighting
- Key light slowly scans through pages.
- One clause glows **red** when conflict appears.
- Rim light emphasizes fracture planes.

### Material Library Usage
- Translucent paper pages as layered glass-like materials (but not glass everywhere).
- Energy/energy threads for neural pathways.

### Typography Reveal Rule (critical)
- **Do not** show “LAWLENS-X” early.
- Only reveal title at climax: after five agents rebuild in real time.

### Screenshots Stage Plan (max 1–2 planes)
- After architecture reveals: one full-bleed plane showing the system output visualization.
- The plane behaves like a hologram panel inside the world.

### Technology/Process Visualization (in-world)
Represent (from resume):
- multi-agent architecture: ClauseParser, RiskAnalyzer, RiskScorer, ScenarioSimulator, NegotiationAdvisor
- hybrid deterministic + LLM contextual
- SSE streaming as a “token stream” ribbon across a hologram wall

(Implementation details forbidden now; keep this as visual metaphor spec.)

### Transition Behavior to next world
- Token stream slows.
- Contract pages liquefy into particles.
- Signature object “absorbs” fragments and forms a new signature state.
- Fog becomes the camera passage into the next environment.

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** contracts gain subtle illumination; clause fragments drift toward/away from cursor.
- **Scroll:** increases agent formation intensity; at scene boundary triggers fog curtain and camera shift.

## 4. Triage Assistant World (Emergency Prioritization)

### Purpose
Emotionally communicate: **emergency assessment becomes prioritization through retrieval + rules.**

### Emotion Arc
Darkness → heartbeat → instability → reorganization → stability → urgency clarity.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- Near darkness with a faint heartbeat pulse (visual-only if audio disabled).
- Distant tri-color lights appear slowly: green, yellow, red.

### Architecture
- Neural pathway represented as a controlled glowing tube network.
- The “retrieval” system appears as a reconfiguring map.

### Shape Language
- Path begins unstable (wobbling beams).
- Incorrect routes collapse.
- Correct route locks into place as smooth rails.

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles: medical motes, denser near the pathway.
- Fog: acts as the “uncertainty layer.”
- Sky: deep emerald/amber tinted darkness.

### Lighting
- Triage thresholds become lighting regions:
  - green = safe routing glow
  - yellow = caution glow
  - red = critical pressure light
- Bloom remains controlled, not neon.

### Material Usage
- Energy threads and liquid-like light volumes.
- Metal minimal; use it only as “frame edges” for stability.

### Typography Reveal Rule
- **Do not** reveal “TRIAGE ASSISTANT” until the neural pathway becomes stable and the triage decision locks.

### In-world Technology Visualization
From resume, represent metaphorically:
- ChromaDB retrieval as semantic beams
- sentence-transformers similarity as aligning vectors
- rule-based clinical reasoning as crisp decision lines
- sub-500ms goal shown as an “execution cadence” (not a timeline widget)

### Screenshots Stage Plan
- One plane showing triage outcome state as a hologram.
- Must feel like medical interface signage, not a dashboard card.

### Transition Behavior
- Unstable pathway collapses into mist.
- Signature object absorbs the three threshold lights and changes its internal “channels.”

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** heart-beat lighting subtly affects pathway intensity.
- **Scroll:** gradually increases system confidence until stability; ends with camera passage into next world.

## 5. Workplace Operations Agent World (Deterministic Orchestration)

### Purpose
Emotionally communicate: **AI organizes chaos into deterministic outcomes across email, Slack, and calendar.**

### Emotion Arc
Chaos → collision → ordering → alignment → deterministic confidence.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- Floating emails, Slack fragments, calendar events represented as layered message rectangles (not UI cards).
- They drift, collide, and partially disintegrate in conflict moments.

### Architecture
- Three “channels” in space:
  - Email channel
  - Slack incident response channel
  - Calendar scheduling channel
- Channels feed a deterministic “grading nucleus.”

### Shape Language
- Early: overlapped streams, crossed lines.
- Mid: streams align onto rails.
- Late: streams become synchronized threads.

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles represent message fragments.
- Fog density increases during conflict (overload), decreases when organized.
- Sky: violet/electric-blue controlled gradient.

### Lighting
- Protocol lighting: aligned beams and stable rim light.
- During chaos: light direction is inconsistent.
- During resolution: light becomes aligned and steady.

### Materials
- Dark alloy metal frames.
- Energy threads for WebSocket-like twin beams metaphor.
- Glass used rarely as “transmission panes.”

### Typography Reveal Rule
- Reveal “WORKPLACE OPERATIONS AGENT” when streams align and deterministic outcome locks.

### In-world Technology Visualization
- deterministic grading system represented as scoring glyphs engraved into the world
- reward shaping represented as partial credit light segments (not charts)
- difficulty levels represented as three rails with different constraint spacing (visual metaphor)

### Transition Behavior
- The grading nucleus compresses streams into the signature object.
- Then the world disassembles into structured fragments that become the next project’s fog curtain.

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** subtly changes beam alignment.
- **Scroll:** increases alignment percentage; at cut triggers chaos dissolving.

## 6. BenefitBridge World (Eligibility Matching & Accessibility)

### Purpose
Emotionally communicate: **eligibility becomes accessible connections, transforming isolation into matching paths.**

### Emotion Arc
Isolation → disconnected points → discovery → connection → accessibility.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- Distant points of light represent people.
- Government forms float through fog like weighty paper masses.
- Early connections missing; later, pathways illuminate.

### Architecture
- A monumental matching lattice in the mid layer.
- Forms flow around it like orbiting documents.

### Shape Language
- Start with scattered points.
- Introduce pathways that “snap” into coherence.
- Final form: a connected web (without looking like a graph UI).

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles: doc dust in fog.
- Fog: heavy early, clears as matching works.
- Sky: emerald/amber civic glow gradient.

### Lighting
- “Civic glow” is calm and stable.
- Eligibility paths illuminate with warm-green/amber light.

### Materials
- Metal framework minimal.
- Forms as stone/paper hybrid materials.

### Typography Reveal Rule
- Reveal title only after multiple eligibility pathways connect and community points brighten.

### In-world Technology Visualization
From resume:
- matching algorithm scoring multiple welfare schemes against criteria
- role-based paths (user vs admin) represented as two branching arcs
- JWT REST APIs represented as request corridors of light (no UI)
- 1,000+ users represented as a constellation count (metaphor, not stats widget)

### Screenshots Stage Plan
- One hero plane showing matching results as an illuminated corridor view.

### Transition Behavior
- The connectivity lattice collapses into signature object state.
- Fog becomes the camera passage into the next technical world.

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** attracts forms slightly; points brighten under proximity.
- **Scroll:** increases matching coherence; transitions at world cut.

## 7. Mavira OS World (Hardened Linux Reliability)

### Purpose
Emotionally communicate: **stability replaces fragmentation through security barriers and core assembly.**

### Emotion Arc
Instability → fragmentation → metallic assembly → security calm → silence.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- Glitches as signal distortion (never flashy).
- Boot sequence metaphors: broken boot lines, corrupted fragments.
- Early: jittering geometry.
- Late: silence returns with heavy stillness.

### Architecture
- World built around a “kernel core” monolith forming from pieces.
- AppArmor policies as transparent enforcement barriers.
- Fail2Ban as detection sparks (rare, precise).

### Shape Language
- Start fractured: broken geometry planes.
- End: integrated monolith with clean seams.

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles: glitch dust motes.
- Fog: used like error masking early; reduced later.
- Sky: cool steel + cyan.

### Lighting
- Early lighting: inconsistent, harsh, and unstable.
- Mid: light stabilizes and locks to monolith axes.
- Late: soft rim light and controlled bloom.

### Materials
- Metal + stone dominate.
- Glass only for policy barriers.

### Typography Reveal Rule
- Reveal “MAVIRA OS” only when metallic core is fully assembled and barriers hold.

### In-world Technology Visualization
Represent:
- Debian live-build origin as layered boot strata
- AppArmor policies as translucent walls with minimal emission
- Fail2Ban as precise detection flare
- performance/usability optimization as reduction of ambient noise (visual calm)

### Transition Behavior
- Glitches become a calm frequency.
- Signature object absorbs the stability pattern.
- Geometry dissolves into next world’s fog.

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** stabilizes/destabilizes signal slightly.
- **Scroll:** increases assembly percentage.

## 8. Personal Health Coach World (Warm Calm & Memory Compression)

### Purpose
Emotionally communicate: **fragmented memories compress into coherent guidance—calm replaces cold darkness.**

### Emotion Arc
Fragmentation → gentle compression → warmth → continuous knowledge flow → peace.

### Atmosphere / Weather
- Floating memory shards drift independently.
- Cold darkness early.
- Warm lighting arrives as shards compress and reconnect.

### Architecture
- A quiet “knowledge chamber” with a central receiving volume.
- Agents represented as cooperative threads, not humanoid UI.

### Shape Language
- Shards (broken geometry) → compressing folds (curved surfaces) → merged ribbon.
- Final: calm, coherent geometry.

### Particles / Fog / Sky
- Particles: shard dust.
- Fog: acts as comforting volume, not obstruction.
- Sky: rose + deep cyan warm blend.

### Lighting
- Warm key light slowly increases.
- Emission becomes soft and steady.
- Bloom remains controlled, supportive of readability.

### Materials
- Liquid-light volumes for streaming responses.
- Glass minimal; use it as gentle boundaries around knowledge chamber.

### Typography Reveal Rule
- Reveal “PERSONAL HEALTH COACH” only after shards compress and a continuous knowledge ribbon forms.

### In-world Technology Visualization
From resume:
- memory compression
- cost-aware execution (represented as restrained energy expenditure—flow never overdraws)
- streaming responses as smooth ribbon flow

### Screenshots Stage Plan
- One plane showing an “ongoing response ribbon” (conceptual), not a chat UI.

### Transition Behavior (to Ending)
- The ribbon steadies.
- World quiets.
- Signature object becomes fully complete.
- Camera begins to circle slowly.

### Interactive Behavior
- **Mouse:** controls warmth intensity slightly.
- **Scroll:** increases coherence; ends with a calm hold moment.

## 9. Ending Environment (Signature Completion & Next Chapter)

### Purpose
Close the film with silence, confidence, and restraint.

### Emotion
Resolution → quiet confidence → anticipation.

### Environment
- Background becomes minimal.
- Signature object stands fully completed.
- Minimal particles drift slowly.

### Lighting
- A single stable key light from a consistent direction.
- Very soft rim light.
- Fog extremely low—clarity.

### Composition
- Negative space dominates.
- Signature object centered or slightly off-center with monumental typography.

### Typography / Text Actions
- One sentence appears only after the camera leaves the final world:
  - “The next chapter is waiting to be built.”

Then after several seconds (understated):
- Resume
- GitHub
- LinkedIn
- Email

No forms, no large buttons, no marketing language.

### Transition Behavior
None—ending is an emotional exhale.

### Interactive Behavior
- Reduced motion mode: camera holds.
- Default: camera slowly circles the completed signature object.

## 10. Validation Checklist (Design Review)
For every environment:
1. Does it feel like a movie set?
2. Does it communicate the story emotionally before any technical details?
3. Is the signature object present or referenced during transitions?
4. Is lighting emotionally consistent with the project?
5. Does fog behave as a boundary, not a cheap overlay?
6. Does typography emerge from fog or light resolve (never slides)?
7. Can this scene be remembered a week later?
